# Governance Module

## Purpose

The Governance Module handles decision-making, authority management, roles, and change processes for cooperation infrastructure and federated organizations.

## Problem

Organizations tend to accumulate power, opacity, corruption, and empty rituals as they grow. This design needs transparent, exit-compatible, and forkable governance.

## Scope

- Decision-making
- Authority management
- Roles
- Voting
- Proposals
- Decisions
- Fork procedures
- Review and appeals

## Out of Scope

- Control of personal beliefs
- Forced participation
- Permanent privileged classes
- Irreversible closed-door decisions

## Relationship to Other Modules

Governance connects to all modules because it defines how rules change. It should not arbitrarily control welfare or economic access.

## Initial Notes

Early governance should use Issues, Pull Requests, Proposals, and Decisions as a lightweight process.

## Open Questions

- How can decision speed and transparency be balanced?
- How can expertise and democratic legitimacy be balanced?
- How should corrupt administration be detected?
