# Arbitration Module

## Purpose

The Arbitration Module handles dispute resolution through transparent procedures rather than violence.

## Problem

Conflicts between states, organizations, or participants often depend on coercive force. Acecore explores lower-cost, recorded, reviewable dispute procedures.

## Scope

- Dispute intake
- Arbitration procedures
- Evidence handling
- Transparent judgments
- Appeals and review
- Inter-organization disputes
- Decision rationale records

## Out of Scope

- Violent enforcement
- Complete replacement of state courts
- Closed-door trials
- Punishment without participant rights

## Relationship to Other Modules

Arbitration connects to governance appeals, identity, economy, welfare, and infrastructure logs.

## Initial Notes

Early work should begin with repository discussions, rule conflicts, and design disputes before attempting legal or real-world arbitration.
