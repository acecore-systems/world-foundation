# Welfare Module

## Purpose

The Welfare Module handles life support that reduces survival anxiety and helps people focus on voluntary activity.

## Problem

Many people accept unwanted work or environments simply to survive. This design explores shared life foundations that increase freedom rather than dependency.

## Scope

- Food
- Housing
- Connectivity
- Education
- Health support
- Access to life resources
- Mutual aid

## Out of Scope

- Forced communal living
- Welfare systems that block exit
- Dependence on a single ideology
- Obedience requirements attached to support

## Relationship to Other Modules

Welfare connects to economy, identity, governance, and arbitration.

## Initial Notes

Early experiments should start with low-risk mutual aid, shared purchasing, cost reduction, and learning support.

## Open Questions

- Where is the boundary between support and dependency?
- How should fairness be designed?
- How can abuse be prevented?
